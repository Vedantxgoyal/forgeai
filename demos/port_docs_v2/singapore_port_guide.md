# Singapore Port Call Guide (fictional training document)

## 1. Pre-arrival Notice
Vessels must submit the arrival declaration at least 12 hours before arrival. Dangerous cargo must be declared at least 24 hours before arrival.

## 2. Pilotage
Pilotage is compulsory for vessels of 300 gross tonnage and above. The pilot is requested at least 12 hours in advance through the Port Operations Control Centre.

## 3. Anchorage
Vessels must anchor only in designated anchorages and keep a continuous listening watch on VHF channel 12.

## 4. Weather Limits
Bunkering is suspended during lightning warnings and when wind speed exceeds 45 km/h.

## 5. Crew Shore Leave
Shore passes are required for all crew going ashore. Crew must return on board at least 2 hours before the scheduled departure.
