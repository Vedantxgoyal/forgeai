# Rotterdam Port Call Guide (fictional training document)

## 1. Pre-arrival Notice
Vessels must send the pre-arrival notification at least 24 hours before arrival. Dangerous cargo must be declared at least 48 hours before arrival.

## 2. Pilotage
Pilotage is compulsory for vessels longer than 75 metres. The pilot boards at the Maas Center pilot station. Request the pilot 6 hours before the estimated time of arrival on VHF channel 2.

## 3. Berthing and Mooring
Vessels over 200 metres require at least 2 tugs. Mooring lines must be tended at all times during cargo operations.

## 4. Weather Limits
Cargo operations at container berths stop when wind speed exceeds 50 km/h. Tug assistance is mandatory when wind speed exceeds 40 km/h.

## 5. Waste and Bunkering
Garbage must be landed before departure unless storage capacity is sufficient for the next voyage. Bunkering requires a completed safety checklist signed by the Chief Engineer.
